## Learning outcomes

- Decide whether a task belongs as a skill, a routine, or a one-off.
- Author a focused skill with clear inputs, outputs, and safety notes.
- Wire the skill into a bot team so the right specialist can call it.
- Harden or retire skills that are too broad, too powerful, or broken.
